Our Story

Built different.
For those who play different.

Where Voltx came from.

Voltx didn't start in a warehouse or a factory. It started with a controller, a blank front plate, and the feeling that everything looked the same. Generic setups. Same colours. Same energy.

We built Voltx to fix that. Every sticker and decal we make is designed from scratch — precision cut, colour-accurate, and made to actually last. We're not here to flood the market with filler. We drop clean, purposeful product and we stand behind every single one.

If you care about how your setup looks, Voltx is for you.

"We drop clean, purposeful product and stand behind every single one."

What we stand for

Built to Last

Every sticker is printed on premium weatherproof vinyl that holds up to sun, rain, and everything in between. 3+ year fade resistance, guaranteed.

Clean by Design

No cluttered graphics. No cheap prints. Every design is stripped back to what matters — sharp lines, bold colour, zero filler. Precision die-cut for a clean edge every time.

Small Drops, High Quality

We keep our runs limited so quality never slips. When we drop something, it's been tested, refined, and signed off. No rush. No corners cut.

Community First

Voltx exists because of the people who use it. We work with content creators and players who actually rep the brand — not just for clout, but because they believe in it.
